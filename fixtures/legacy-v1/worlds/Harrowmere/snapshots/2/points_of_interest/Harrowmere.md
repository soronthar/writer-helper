# Harrowmere

## Overview
- **Type:** City
- **Located in:** The Marrowfen Reach
- **Size / Scale:** Major port
- **Controlled by:** Harbor Guild

## Description
A city of brackish canals and stilt-houses where every bridge has a toll and every quay a clerk. The air smells of tallow smoke and wet rope.

## History
Founded on the Charter of Three Seals, which divided the harbor between guild, union and wardens.
