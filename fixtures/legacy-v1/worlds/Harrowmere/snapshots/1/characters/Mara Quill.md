# Mara Quill

## Identity
- **Full Name:** Mara Quill
- **Age:** 34
- **Gender:** Female
- **Occupation / Role:** Ledger-keeper at the Tally House

## Appearance
Narrow, ink-stained hands; a grey wool coat she never takes off in the Tally House; hair pinned with a pencil.

## Personality
Methodical and dry-humoured. Cannot leave an unanswered question alone. Distrusts anything that adds up too neatly. Afraid of being the person who looked away.

## Background
Born in the Flats to a family of reed-cutters. Trained as a tallier at fourteen and has kept the Guild's tallow-tax ledger for eleven years.
