# Chapter 2 — Forty-Seven Casks

Forty-seven casks of tallow had been entered in the ledger and never landed. Mara counted them by the quay-tally, by the toll-book and by the barge-masters' slips, and the three sources agreed with each other and with nothing else.

Ilsa Brandt found her at the lower quay at dusk, folding rule in hand, and asked how the work was going as though she did not already know.
