# Active Schemes — <Story Name>

> **What this is:** a live registry of the deceptions, concealments, manipulations, and unresolved operational commitments currently in flight within this story. Character bibles describe who a character *is*; this file tracks what they are currently *doing* — the operational details that otherwise live only in prose and are invisible to downstream agents.
>
> **Maintained by:** Story Analyzer (updates after each chapter).
> **Read by:** Planner (first input, before outline), Draft Writer, Reviewer, Draft Fixer.
>
> **Status values:**
> - `Active` — in motion, the owner's position depends on the deception holding.
> - `Complicated` — partial slippage; one or more targets suspect, or a precondition is failing.
> - `Exposed` — the deception is known to its target(s); moves to Resolution Log.
> - `Resolved` — concluded on the owner's terms (paid off, quietly abandoned, absorbed into a larger scheme); moves to Resolution Log.
>
> **Scope:** story-local. Schemes that survive past the end of this story should be lifted into the next story's ACTIVE_SCHEMES.md or into HOOKS.md during World Archivist → Consolidate.
