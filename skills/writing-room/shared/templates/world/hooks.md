# Story Hooks — <World Name>

Story hooks are narrative seeds: situations, tensions, questions, or character dynamics that could grow into a story. They are collected here so the Story Ideator, Planner, and Writing Room Head have a single place to browse for inspiration.

**Who adds hooks:** The user (at any time) and the Story Analyzer (after a chapter is completed, if events reveal new possibilities).
**Status values:** `Open` · `In Use` · `Archived`
