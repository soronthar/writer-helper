## World Overview

Harrowmere is a damp fenland port-republic where accounts matter more than swords, with no magic and power held by whoever keeps the records. Three bodies share the harbor under the Charter of Three Seals.

## Active Tensions

The last harbor-master died without an heir and Hesk Varrow holds the seal as acting head until the Guild votes. A replaced page in the tallow ledger has made every record suspect, and the Union is buying influence in the Guild offices. Mara Quill has seen the sealed original, and Hesk Varrow knows it.
