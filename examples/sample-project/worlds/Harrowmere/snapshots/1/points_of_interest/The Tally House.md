# The Tally House

## Overview
- **Type:** Building
- **Located in:** Harrowmere
- **Size / Scale:** Large
- **Controlled by:** Harbor Guild

## Description
A long brick hall with narrow windows, built so that no ledger ever sits in direct sun. Corridors of shelved volumes lead to a sealed room at the back.

## History
Raised in the first year of the Charter to hold the three seals' accounts.

## Notable Features
- The records room
- The sealed room
- The ledger desk
